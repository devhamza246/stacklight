# T-1002: Webhook endpoint returning 429

Customer: Harbor Logistics
Plan: Business
Priority: P2
Opened: 2026-09-05
Status: Resolved

## Customer report
Incoming webhooks to our flow trigger are intermittently rejected with HTTP 429.

## Resolution
Their sender bursts above 100 requests per second per workspace, which is the incoming webhook cap. Advised batching events; customer added a queue upstream. Closed.
