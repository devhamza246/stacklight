# T-1025: Rate limit on Team too low

Customer: Hollis Data
Plan: Team
Priority: P3
Opened: 2026-10-05
Status: Resolved

## Customer report
Our sync hits 429 at 300 requests per minute.

## Resolution
Team limit is 300 requests per minute; Business 1,200. Recommended backoff using Retry-After and batching, or upgrading to Business.
