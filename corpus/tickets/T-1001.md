# T-1001: Run quota reached banner on Free workspace

Customer: Fernwood Studio
Plan: Free
Priority: P3
Opened: 2026-09-03
Status: Resolved

## Customer report
Our flows stopped and we see 'Run quota reached' on the 24th. We only run about 900 runs a day.

## Resolution
Free includes 1,000 runs per month, not per day. Explained that flows resume automatically on the 1st at 00:00 UTC and suggested Team (50,000 pooled runs). Customer upgraded.
