# T-1038: Webhook signature verification failing

Customer: Juniper Pay
Plan: Business
Priority: P3
Opened: 2026-10-21
Status: Resolved

## Customer report
HMAC verification fails in our endpoint.

## Resolution
They hashed the parsed JSON instead of the raw body. Signature is HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body using the endpoint secret in X-Quillbase-Signature. Fixed on their side.
