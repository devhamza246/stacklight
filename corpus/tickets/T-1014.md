Customer: Cobalt Retail
Plan: Business
Priority: P3
Opened: 2026-09-21
Status: Resolved

## Customer report
We expected more retries for failed deliveries.

## Resolution
Outgoing webhooks retry 5 times with exponential backoff, then mark failed. Events can be replayed manually for 7 days; showed the replay button.
