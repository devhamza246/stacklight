# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-03-30

- Quillbase is remote-first. There are no mandatory office days.
- Core collaboration hours are 15:00 to 18:00 UTC (overlapping the US and Europe). Meetings are scheduled inside this window where possible.
- Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval; longer stays require People Operations review for tax and legal reasons.
- Team offsites happen twice a year (spring and autumn) and travel is paid by the company.
- Company-issued devices must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the company VPN enabled.
