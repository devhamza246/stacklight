# Billing FAQ

Owner: Customer Success
Last reviewed: 2026-07-21

Answers to the billing questions customers ask most often.

## When am I charged?
Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month as your signup. Annual plans are charged once on the anniversary date. Overage for extra runs is charged on the first business day after each period closes.

## Can I get a refund?
Yes, in the following cases:
- **Refund window:** 14 days from the date of the charge, for monthly and annual plans.
- Annual plans are refundable only if the workspace has used fewer than 1,000 runs since the charge.
- Refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.
- Overage charges and add-ons are not refundable.

## Can I change plans mid-cycle?
Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a pro-rated amount for the rest of the period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

## Which currencies do you bill in?
USD, EUR and GBP. Your currency is fixed when the first invoice is issued.

## Where do I find invoices?
Under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Invoices are available as PDF and carry your VAT ID if you have provided one.
