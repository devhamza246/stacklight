# PTO and Holidays

Owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-02-16

- Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year; the rest expire on 31 March.
- Quillbase observes 10 company holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, and 24 to 25 December.
- Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, no doctor's note needed for absences under 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for the birth or adoption of a child, for all parents. It can be taken any time in the first 12 months.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family.
- Request PTO in the HR system at least 2 weeks ahead for absences over 5 days. Managers respond within 2 business days.
- Employees are encouraged to take at least 10 consecutive days off once a year.
