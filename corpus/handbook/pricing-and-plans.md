# Pricing and Plans

Owner: Product Marketing
Last reviewed: 2026-09-14

Quillbase is a workflow automation platform. A *flow* is a trigger plus up to 50 steps; a *run* is one execution of a flow. Prices below are in USD, per seat per month.

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual price (billed yearly) | Seats | Included runs per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | up to 3 | 1,000 |
| Team | $29 | $24 | 2 to 25 | 50,000 (pooled across the workspace) |
| Business | $79 | $66 | minimum 10, no maximum | 500,000 (pooled) |
| Enterprise | custom | custom | unlimited | custom, 5,000,000 or more |

## Notes
- Annual billing is a 17% discount versus monthly, roughly two months free.
- Extra runs on Team and Business are billed at $2.00 per additional 1,000 runs, charged in arrears at the end of the billing period.
- Business includes SSO (SAML and OIDC), audit logs and priority support. SCIM provisioning is Enterprise only.
- Nonprofits and accredited schools get 30% off Team and Business; apply through sales.
- All prices exclude taxes. VAT is added for EU customers.
- A 21-day free trial of Business is available to any new workspace, no card required.
