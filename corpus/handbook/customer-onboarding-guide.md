# Customer Onboarding Guide

Owner: Customer Success
Last reviewed: 2026-09-30

New workspaces follow five steps. Most teams finish in under a day.

1. **Create the workspace.** Choose US-East or EU-Frankfurt; this cannot be changed later.
2. **Connect your first integration.** Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, GitHub, Jira, Zendesk, Google Sheets or Snowflake.
3. **Build a first flow** from a template. The "New lead to Slack alert" template is the most popular and takes about 10 minutes.
4. **Invite teammates.** Roles are Owner, Admin, Editor and Viewer. Viewers are free and do not count as seats on paid plans.
5. **Turn on run alerts** under Settings > Notifications so failures email the flow owner.

## Help available
- Free and Team: self-serve docs and a live onboarding webinar every Tuesday at 16:00 UTC (about 45 minutes).
- Business: a kickoff call offered within 3 business days of purchase, plus one 60-minute workshop.
- Enterprise: a named Customer Success Manager and a 30-day onboarding plan (see the Enterprise Onboarding Playbook).

Success is measured by the "activation" milestone: 3 flows running successfully within the first 14 days.
