# Expense Policy

Owner: Finance
Last reviewed: 2026-04-27

- Pre-approval from your manager is needed for any single expense over $250.
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase; reports older than 60 days are not reimbursed.
- Itemised receipts are required for every expense above $25.
- Flights: book economy for trips under 6 hours and premium economy for longer ones, at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- Hotels: up to $220 per night in major cities, $160 elsewhere.
- Meals while traveling: up to $75 per day. Client meals: up to $100 per person with VP approval.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only with clients and capped at 2 drinks per person.
- Reimbursements are paid in the next bi-weekly payroll after approval.
- Company cards are issued to Directors and above, and to Sales for client entertainment; card statements are reconciled by the 5th of each month.
