# Enterprise Onboarding Playbook

Owner: Customer Success
Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

Internal playbook for Customer Success Managers (CSMs) handling Enterprise accounts.

## Timeline (30 days)
- **Day 0 to 3:** kickoff call with the executive sponsor and technical lead; agree success criteria in writing.
- **Day 4 to 10:** SSO and SCIM configured; security review questions closed; data region confirmed.
- **Day 11 to 20:** first three production flows built with Solutions Engineering; integrations connected.
- **Day 21 to 30:** admin training session (90 minutes), runbook handoff, and the 30-day review.

## Rules
- Every Enterprise account has one named CSM and one backup CSM.
- A CSM manages at most 15 Enterprise accounts.
- Health score is reviewed weekly: green means 3 or more active flows and weekly logins; red means no successful run in 14 days.
- Executive business reviews (EBRs) are held quarterly.
- Escalate blockers older than 5 business days to the Director of Customer Success.
