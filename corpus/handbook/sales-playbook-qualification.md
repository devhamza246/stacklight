# Sales Playbook: Qualification

Owner: Sales Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-09-17

We qualify with BANT plus a security check.

- **Budget:** Business starts at 10 seats, so a minimum of roughly $7,900 per year on annual billing (10 x $66 x 12).
- **Authority:** identify the economic buyer; for Enterprise, involve the VP-level sponsor by the second call.
- **Need:** at least 2 manual workflows costing 10 or more hours per week.
- **Timeline:** a decision expected within 90 days.

## Pipeline stages
Prospect, Discovery, Demo, Security Review, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed. Opportunities with no activity for 21 days are flagged stale.

## Handoffs
- Marketing qualified leads are called within 15 minutes during business hours.
- Deals above $50,000 in annual contract value (ACV) require a Solutions Engineer on the demo.
- Companies with fewer than 10 employees are routed to self-serve unless they are flagged strategic.
- Security questionnaires are returned within 5 business days; use the standard SIG-lite response document.
- Trials: a 21-day Business trial can be extended once by 7 days by an account executive.
