# Competitive Battlecard

Owner: Product Marketing
Last reviewed: 2026-08-31

Use only claims you can support. Competitor names below are generic placeholders for the demo.

## Versus "FlowMaster" (legacy incumbent)
- Strengths: large integration catalog (400+), brand recognition.
- Weaknesses: per-task pricing becomes expensive above 100,000 tasks per month; no EU data region.
- Our pitch: predictable per-seat pricing, EU-Frankfurt hosting, and 365-day run logs on Business.

## Versus "AutoLoop" (startup)
- Strengths: slick visual editor, aggressive free tier.
- Weaknesses: no SOC 2 report, no SSO below its top tier.
- Our pitch: SOC 2 Type II today, SSO from Business, and 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Objection handling
- "You have fewer integrations": we cover 8 native integrations plus webhook and HTTP steps; most customers need 3 or fewer.
- "Too expensive": show the annual price of $66 per seat on Business, which includes 500,000 runs.
- Never disparage competitors in writing, and never share this document externally.
