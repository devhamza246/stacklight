# Sales: Discounting and Quotes

Owner: Sales Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-09-17

## Discount authority (off list price)
- Account Executives: up to 10%.
- Sales Managers: up to 20%.
- VP of Sales: up to 30%. Anything higher needs CFO approval.
- Discounts stack with the standard 17% annual-billing discount only up to a combined 35%.
- Multi-year deals: 5% extra for a 2-year commitment, 8% extra for a 3-year commitment.

## Quotes
- Quotes are valid for 30 days.
- Payment terms: Net 30 for annual contracts; monthly contracts are card-only.
- Non-standard terms (custom liability caps, uncapped indemnity) go to Legal; Legal's turnaround target is 3 business days.
- Commission: 10% of first-year ACV for new business, 3% for renewals. Quota attainment is measured quarterly.
- Pilot programs are allowed for up to 60 days and 25 seats, at no charge, with a signed pilot agreement.
