# Benefits and Stipends

Owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-03-09

- Health, dental and vision: Quillbase pays 90% of employee premiums and 70% of dependent premiums.
- Retirement: 401(k) with a 4% company match, vested immediately.
- Learning stipend: $1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences.
- Home office stipend: $750 one-time at hire, plus $50 per month for internet.
- Wellness stipend: $75 per month for gym or mental-health apps.
- Equipment: a laptop is refreshed every 36 months; choose a Mac or Linux machine.
- Co-working: up to $300 per month if you do not have a suitable place at home.
- Employee Assistance Program: 6 free counseling sessions per issue, confidential.
- Benefits begin on the first day of the month after your start date.
