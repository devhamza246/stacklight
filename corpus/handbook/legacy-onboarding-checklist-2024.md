# Customer Onboarding Checklist (2024 Edition)

Owner: Customer Success
Last reviewed: 2024-08-12

Checklist for onboarding a new Quillbase customer. Follow in order.

1. Ask the customer to email support@quillbase.example to request an API key; keys are created manually by the support team within 2 business days.
2. Confirm the plan. The Team plan costs $19 per seat per month, billed monthly, with a limit of 10,000 runs a month. The Starter plan is $9 per seat.
3. Add the customer to the shared `#customer-hotline` Slack channel and to the weekly Thursday webinar at 15:00 UTC.
4. Set up integrations using API v1. Use the base URL `https://api.quillbase.example/v1` and the `X-Api-Key` header for authentication.
5. Run logs are retained for 14 days on all paid plans.
6. For security questions send the customer the PDF "Security Whitepaper 2023", which describes our ISO 27001 and HIPAA readiness.
7. After 30 days book a feedback call with the account manager, Pat from Sales Ops.

Sign off the checklist in the CRM when all 7 items are done.
