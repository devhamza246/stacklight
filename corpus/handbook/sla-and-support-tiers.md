# SLA and Support Tiers

Owner: Customer Support
Last reviewed: 2026-09-22

## Uptime commitment
| Plan | Monthly uptime SLA |
|---|---|
| Free | none |
| Team | none (target 99.5%) |
| Business | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% |

Service credits for Business and Enterprise: uptime below the SLA but at least 99.0% earns 10% of that month's fee; below 99.0% but at least 95.0% earns 25%; below 95.0% earns 50%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the month by emailing support. Scheduled maintenance (announced 5 days ahead, at most 4 hours a month) is excluded.

## Support channels and first-response targets
| Plan | Channels | Standard first response | P1 first response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | community forum | none | none |
| Team | email | 24 business hours | 8 business hours |
| Business | email and chat | 4 business hours | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| Enterprise | email, chat, phone, shared Slack channel | 2 business hours | 15 minutes, 24/7 |

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 UTC Monday to Friday, excluding company holidays.

## Severity definitions
- **P1:** production flows are down or data is being lost for most users.
- **P2:** major feature degraded, workaround exists.
- **P3:** minor issue or question.
- **P4:** feature request or documentation feedback.
