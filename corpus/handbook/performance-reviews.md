# Performance Reviews

Owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-06-02

- Reviews happen twice a year: in May (covering November to April) and in November (covering May to October).
- Each review includes a self-assessment, 3 to 5 peer reviews, and a manager assessment, rated on a 1 to 4 scale (1 = below expectations, 4 = exceptional).
- Salary adjustments are decided once a year, effective 1 January, based on the November review. Promotions can happen at any time.
- Review calibration is held by Directors within 10 business days after the review window closes.
- An employee rated 1 receives a 60-day performance improvement plan with written goals and biweekly check-ins.
- Engineering levels run from L1 to L7; promotion to L5 (Senior) requires evidence of leading a project across two or more teams.
