# Refund Policy

Owner: Finance and Legal
Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

This policy applies to all paid Quillbase plans.

- **Refund window:** 30 days from the date of your first purchase of a paid plan.
- Within the window you receive a full refund of the amount paid, for both monthly and annual plans, with no minimum or maximum usage condition.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of approval.
- Requests are made by emailing billing@quillbase.example or through the in-app "Billing" > "Request refund" button.
- Renewals are not covered by the refund window. If you were charged for a renewal and did not intend to renew, contact support within 72 hours of the charge and we will review it as a goodwill exception.
- Overage charges for extra runs are never refundable.
- Enterprise contracts follow the terms of the signed order form instead of this policy.

Support agents may approve refunds up to $500. Larger amounts require a support manager, and refunds above $5,000 require Finance sign-off (see the Support Escalation Runbook).
