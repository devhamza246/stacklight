# New Employee Onboarding

Owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-05-12

- The laptop ships 5 business days before the start date. IT sends account invites on day 1 at 09:00 in your time zone.
- Every new hire gets an onboarding buddy (not their manager) for the first 90 days.
- **Week 1:** security training (2 hours, must be completed by day 5), product walkthrough, and meetings with each team lead.
- **30-60-90 plan:** your manager writes it with you in week 1. At 30 days you ship something small; at 60 days you own a project; at 90 days you present results.
- Everyone, including engineers, completes 2 shadowing sessions with Support in the first month.
- Hardware security keys are issued on day 1; set up SSO and the key before accessing production systems.
- There is a company all-hands every second Thursday at 17:00 UTC.
- Probationary check-ins happen at 30 and 90 days; there is no formal probation period.
