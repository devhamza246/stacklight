# Invoicing and Payment Methods

Owner: Finance
Last reviewed: 2026-06-10

- Self-serve plans (Free, Team, Business) pay by credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Business annual plans may also pay by ACH or wire transfer.
- Enterprise customers are invoiced with Net 30 terms. Invoices past due by 15 days trigger a reminder; at 45 days past due the workspace is placed in read-only mode.
- When a card payment fails we retry on day 3, day 5 and day 7. After the third failure the workspace is downgraded to Free limits and flows above the Free limits are paused, not deleted.
- Purchase orders are accepted for annual contracts of $10,000 or more.
- Tax: Quillbase collects sales tax in US states where it has nexus, and VAT in the EU and UK. Customers can add a tax ID in Settings > Billing.
- Invoice questions go to billing@quillbase.example; the target response time is 2 business days.
